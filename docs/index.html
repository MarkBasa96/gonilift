Take the singer out of any song, and sing it yourself.

Gonilift is a free Windows app that splits any song into vocals and a backing track with AI, right on your own PC. Mute the singer, change the key, slow it down, and loop the hard parts for practice.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Still have questions? Open an issue on GitHub →

Do I need a subscription or account?No. Gonilift is completely free and open source.

The AI is an open-source model that runs on your own computer, not an online service. There is nothing to pay, no sign-up and no API key.

Does it work offline?Yes, after setup and the first model download.

You need internet once to install and once for the first split (the AI model, about 900 MB). After that it works with no internet at all; we tested it with every outside connection blocked.

Are my songs uploaded anywhere?No. Everything happens on your computer.

Gonilift only listens on your own PC and sends nothing anywhere. Your files never leave your computer.

How fast is it?About 40 seconds for a 3-minute song on an RTX 3050.

With an NVIDIA graphics card it is fast. Without one it runs on the processor and takes several minutes per song.

What do I need to run it?Windows 10 or 11 and about 6 GB of space.

An NVIDIA graphics card makes it much faster but isn't required. Closing the app stops everything; nothing runs in the background.

Is it legal?Yes, for personal practice with music you may use.

Splitting a song doesn't give you rights to it, so please don't publish tracks made from songs you don't own.
